About the tool

ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System. Recruiters use it to sort hundreds of resumes by keywords before reading any of them.

This tool does the same check for you. It reads your PDF, finds technical skills, soft skills and tools, and compares them with the job description.

  • Works with text-based PDF resumes up to 5 MB
  • Covers IT roles: development, DevOps, cloud, data and security
  • Use the result to improve your resume, not to add skills you do not have

Common questions

What is a good match score?

Above 60% is a solid start. Below 40% usually means key skills from the job are missing or named differently in your resume.

Should I add every missing keyword?

No. Add only skills you actually have. Interviewers ask about whatever is on your resume.

Why does it say my PDF is unreadable?

Scanned or image-only PDFs have no text to read. Export your resume as PDF from Word or Google Docs instead.

Is my resume stored?

The file is deleted from the server right after it is read.
